01 · About the author

A life written
in chapters.

03
Born on 3 June 2008, Jonathan Brown began writing in Richmond, Britain.

His literary journey expanded after the publication of his first book in Melbourne, Australia, followed by a period of exploration in Sydney.

His work has been described by the author as reaching readers and audiences across the United States, the Netherlands, South Africa, Asia and New Zealand.
