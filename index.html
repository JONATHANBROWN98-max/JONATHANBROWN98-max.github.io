About

A writer
in motion.

Jonathan Brown began writing in Richmond, Britain, developing a body of work shaped by questions of consciousness, human desire, technology and the hidden structures of awareness.

His literary journey later expanded through Australia, beginning with a first publication in Melbourne and continuing through Sydney as an exploratory chapter in his work.

The archive now gathers his books, ideas, essays and places of research into one evolving literary space.
